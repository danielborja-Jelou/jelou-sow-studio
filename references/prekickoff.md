# Pre-kickoff mode (before the SOW)

## Why it exists
The real flow: Sales/Presales hand over an **incomplete pre-SOW** (Word) and the **Diio of the handoff meeting** (what was sold). When much is missing beyond the team, building the full SOW only produces a document full of red. Instead the skill first generates **three things** and then waits for the client meeting:

1. a **pre-kickoff deck** (`.pptx`) to present to the client (format `kickoff` by default, see «Deck format `kickoff`» below; `--formato completo` gives the 22-25 slide version; the 4-phase roadmap is never omitted: slide 3 after the contents in `kickoff`, slide 2 in `completo`);
2. a **faltantes document for the CLIENT** (Word, business language: what they must deliver or decide, owner, date);
3. a **faltantes document INTERNAL** (Word, Jelou only: capability checks, presales doubts, assumptions, additionals for Comercial, changes versus presales, team data, the UAT clause pending Legal).

4. the **WhatsApp simulator** (`*-simulador.html`, built with the `jelou-demo-whatsapp` kit through `scripts/sow_a_demo.js`), so the client tries the conversation in the pre-kickoff session itself. Anything the presales input does not define is mocked with probable data and marked in the step notes; the final SOW rebuilds the simulator from the final graph.

Then the user holds the client meeting and brings the **new Diio** with the answers. With pre-SOW + Diio 1 + Diio 2 (+ any inputs) the skill builds the whole package (mode A/C: SOW Word, figures, simulator, Gantt, proposal). If the client does not deliver the technical inputs for v1, mode B versions it (1.1, 1.2…) when they arrive, keeping unresolved items red.

```
pre-SOW + Diio 1 ──► sow.json (meta.fase "prekickoff") ──► umbral_prekickoff.js ──┬─ context enough ─► full SOW (confirm with the user, ask for example resources)
                                                                                   └─ threshold met ──► build_all.js --modo prekickoff
                                                                                        ├─ deck .pptx (present to the client)
                                                                                        ├─ faltantes-cliente.docx (business questions)
                                                                                        ├─ faltantes-tecnico.docx (technical questions, for the client's TI team)
                                                                                        ├─ faltantes-interno.docx
                                                                                        └─ simulator .html (WhatsApp look, from the handoff graph)
client meeting ─► Diio 2 ─► update sow.json (answers; scope lock) ─► remove meta.fase ─► build_all.js ─► full package ─► (no technical inputs yet?) versions 1.1, 1.2…
```

## Activation threshold (deterministic: `node scripts/umbral_prekickoff.js sow.json`)
Besides the team, enter pre-kickoff when ANY of:

| Condition | How it is counted |
|---|---|
| **>= 5 blocking open questions** | `preguntas_abiertas[].bloquea: true`, excluding `tipo: "equipo"` or `tema: "Equipo"` |
| **Integrations without documentation or QA/test environment** | any `integraciones[]` whose `documentacion` or `ambiente_pruebas` is not `si` |
| **Presales assumptions not confirmed by the client** | any `alcance.supuestos` entry that is a string (a `[Supuesto]` of the presales doc) or an object without `confirmado_cliente: true` |

If none applies the script says the context is enough: tell the user, **ask them to confirm** going straight to the full SOW and to **provide example resources** (documents, texts, access), then follow mode A.

## What the skill does, step by step
1. Read the pre-SOW (`references/design-document.md`) and Diio 1; ask the team question (SKILL.md A2).
2. Build a **partial `sow.json`** with `meta.fase: "prekickoff"`: agents with the conversation graph (`Flujo_N`), `integraciones` (with `expone`/`consume`), `preguntas_abiertas` with `audiencia`, **`ambito` (negocio | tecnico: the technical expert usually misses the first meeting, so the deck only asks business questions)**, `tipo`, `etapa`, `tema`, `integracion`; `alcance.supuestos` (objects with the question that closes each), `riesgos`, `dependencias_cliente`, `cronograma` (proposed days), and the `prekickoff` section below. Do not invent figures: pending = `POR COMPLETAR` / `POR DEFINIR` (red).
3. Detect **extras** and **changes versus presales** (below): extras become `ampliaciones_solicitadas` + ONE question to Comercial each; contradictions become `cambios_respecto_preventa` + a question.
4. `node scripts/umbral_prekickoff.js sow.json`, then `node scripts/build_all.js sow.json out --modo prekickoff` (or just set `meta.fase`). The linter is tolerant in this mode (full-SOW sections are not required, client deliverables still missing are warnings) but still checks credentials, graph integrity, contradictions with out of scope, the scope lock and the diagram rules.
5. Look at the deck (render it: PowerPoint/LibreOffice, or open it), and read the faltantes documents. Deliver the files; do not send anything to the client unless the user asks. The technical document is meant to be sent to the client's TI team after the meeting.

## Deck format `kickoff` (default)
Closer to the Jelou kickoff deck the client already knows, with the pre-kickoff design (Poppins, logo top right, eyebrows) and one palette for every slide. `node build_kickoff_pptx.js sow.json out.pptx [--formato kickoff|completo]`, `build_all.js … --formato …`, or `prekickoff.formato` in sow.json.

| # | Slide | Source in sow.json |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover: dark panel with the white logo, client name, «PRE-KICKOFF», project line and date; the person photo on the right (`portada-persona.jpg` in the template) | `meta.cliente`, `prekickoff.portada_cliente`, `portada_etiqueta`, `subtitulo_corto` (fallback `meta.proyecto`), `fecha_sesion` (ISO `2026-10-20` prints 20/10/2026 and dates the next steps; free text is printed as is) |
| 2 | Contents (one row per section present) | automatic; `prekickoff.contenido` [{titulo, texto}] overrides |
| 3 | **Team and escalation** (from the Jelou kickoff): two columns, the client and its partners on the left, Jelou on the right; each person «Nombre · Cargo» (missing names print `POR COMPLETAR` in red, filled live) and the responsibility; escalation note below. No 4-phase roadmap in this format (the project route says the same; `completo` keeps it) | `equipo` (`organizacion` decides the column); `prekickoff.equipo_titulo`, `escalamiento` (text) |
| 4 | **Project route without times**: one chevron per milestone, description, deliverable and owner pill; «Tiempos por definir» note. Days, hours and dates are never printed (they are stripped from names and descriptions too) | `cronograma.hitos` {nombre, descripcion, entregable, responsable}; defaults when empty; `prekickoff.cronograma_titulo`, `cronograma_nota` |
| 4b | **Metodología y seguimiento** («Plan de Acción» rows, right after the route): Reuniones (weekly 30-60 min with the organizations of the team; daily async WhatsApp group), Actas y acuerdos (minutes within 24 h, owner and date per agreement, shared pending log), Reporte de avance (risks and blockers traffic light (ClickUp), milestones approved in writing) | automatic; `prekickoff.metodologia` [{titulo, items[]}], `metodologia_titulo` |
| 5 | Context: three outlined arrow boxes Brief / Objetivos identificados / Roles identificados | `prekickoff.contexto.brief`, `objetivos`, `roles` (arrays); fallbacks `brief`, `objetivos.general` + `especificos`, `prekickoff.actores.items`. Too long: first brief paragraph, then at most 4 objectives / roles, each with a layout notice |
| 6 | **One slide per flow** (from the Jelou kickoff, replaces the validation by stage): the narrative journey, the milestones as chevrons (at most 6, 3 lines each), «Si algo falla: …» (advisor milestones) and a «Preguntas clave» panel with ALL the BUSINESS questions of that flow (blockers first; they are closed in the session): 12 -> 10.5 pt, and when they still do not fit they continue on a «<flujo> (cont.)» slide (11 pt, «Continúa en la siguiente diapositiva →»). The faltantes documents keep them too | journey: `prekickoff.flujos.<id>.recorrido` > `agentes[].descripcion` (without it only the chevrons); chevrons: `prekickoff.flujos.<id>.hitos` > `agentes[].resumen_cliente`; questions: `preguntas_abiertas` (ámbito negocio) by `agente`, else by `etapa` = a `grupo`/`etapa` of the flow, else the first flow; `prekickoff.preguntas_por_flujo` (optional cap; then «+N … en el documento de faltantes») |
| 7 | **Scope: «Incluye»** (one card, two columns, sized to its content; out of scope stays in the SOW and the faltantes) | `prekickoff.alcance.incluye` > `alcance.base` > Jelou stages |
| 7b | **«Alcance: el flujo de punta a punta»**: native diagram of the WHOLE project in the style of the Jelou kickoff flow slides: entry (dark), milestones (mint), a decision diamond (light orange) after every milestone with 2+ exits («Sí» / «No»; the diamond text is the milestone's `pregunta`, else «¿<first exit>?»), advisor (white), end (green edge); orthogonal connectors; the main path in bands of up to 6 columns, branches in a lane below their source, entry chains (outbound) under the node they feed; two bands per slide, numbered connector circles between bands; more -> «(cont.)» slide. **Rule: with two or more flows, every diagram slide carries at least two flows** (only the last one may hold a single flow when the count is odd). Sizes are tried from the largest: «normal» (up to 6 columns), «denso» (up to 8 columns, smaller boxes and gaps), «compacto» (one step smaller); the first that meets the rule wins. If none does (a very long flow), the size with fewer slides wins and the builder prints a notice to shorten `resumen_cliente` | `agentes[].resumen_cliente` of every flow (same graph as the FE figure) + optional `pregunta` per milestone; `prekickoff.e2e` {titulo, eyebrow, nota, densidad: "normal" | "denso" | "compacto" to force a size} |

| … | Validation grouped by stage — **only with `prekickoff.validacion: "etapas"`** (the flow slides above replace it by default) (`validationGrouped`): one "Plan de Acción" row per stage (number block «NN · ETAPA», band = stage name, panel = its questions under small topic headings); consecutive stages share a slide while they fit (at most 3 rows; one question size per slide, 12 -> 10 pt); a stage longer than a slide continues in a «(cont.)» row; the notes box «Notas / respuesta del cliente» on every slide. The arrow rows are used only here. **Business questions only** | `preguntas_abiertas` (audience cliente, `ambito` negocio) by `etapa` then `tema`; `prekickoff.validacion_titulo` |
| … | **«Lo que necesitamos de tu equipo técnico»** (table Qué necesitamos / Quién lo entrega / Para cuándo + note): name the technical contact (in this session), the technical requests and «Responder el documento de preguntas técnicas (N preguntas)». No technical question text. Only when there is something technical to ask or request | `C.solicitudesTecnicas`: `dependencias_cliente` tied to an integration (or with technical wording) + integrations of the client still without documentation / QA (integrations Jelou builds are skipped); `preguntas_abiertas` with `ambito` tecnico (count); `prekickoff.tecnico` {titulo, eyebrow, nota} |
| … | Risks: the cards of `completo` (2x3) with a level pill under the icon (ALTO orange, MEDIO turquoise, BAJO light blue) | `riesgos` {riesgo, mitigacion, nivel \| impacto \| probabilidad} |
| … | **Next steps of the kickoff week** («Plan de Acción» rows): «Hoy» (validate assumptions and key questions, name the technical contact, complete the team), «Dentro de 24 h» (minutes, client faltantes, TI document), «Antes del SOW» (answers to the blocking questions, technical deliverables, SOW v1.0). With an ISO `fecha_sesion` the labels become «Hoy · 20/10», «Al 21/10», «Al 27/10» (business days). They are commitments of the week, not the project schedule | automatic; `prekickoff.proximos_pasos` [{cuando, items[], responsable}], `proximos_titulo` |
| last | «¿Tienes dudas? ¡Conversemos!» with the photo | `prekickoff.cierre_titulo`, `cierre_texto` |

Order: cover, contents, team, route, methodology, context, flows, scope + end-to-end diagram, assumptions, technical team, risks, next steps, closing (about 15 slides for one flow). Not in this format (they stay in `completo` and in the faltantes documents): the 4-phase roadmap, agenda cards, HOY vs CON JELOU, components, actors, end-to-end diagram, «quién llama a quién», outbound, block divider, integrations table, pending items, schedule days and the UAT rule.

Emphasis in titles: `*words*` turn turquoise; `POR COMPLETAR` / `POR DEFINIR` print red.

Rules the builder enforces: in `completo` the roadmap is **never optional and never moves** (always slide 2, right after the cover, with the agenda as slide 3); the `kickoff` format has no roadmap (the project route covers it); an override of `como_trabajamos` must keep exactly 4 phases (linter ERROR `ROAD` otherwise; the builder falls back to the default phases) and each card shrinks its list 12 -> 11 -> 10 -> 9 pt before truncating with a layout notice; every list is measured with the real Poppins metrics and **split into continuation slides** instead of overflowing (validation rows, tables, pending, risks); the font shrinks (12 -> 11 -> 10.5 in cards and validation rows (uniform per slide)) before a card or row is split; a title never exceeds two lines; an end-to-end diagram with more than 6 layers falls back to the engine's picture and the builder prints a notice. Layout notices (`layout notice:` lines) mean something had to be truncated: fix the text.

Validation rows (`validacion-plan`), balanced layout (`layoutPlan` in `build_kickoff_pptx.js`): one row per `tema` of the stage (number = topic order). (1) A topic is never split while it has up to 4 questions and fits one row at 10.5 pt; a longer or taller topic is split into equal halves, titled "... (cont.)" with the same number, never leaving a lone question in a "(cont.)" row. (2) At most 3 rows per slide, spread evenly over the fewest slides (4 rows -> 2+2, 5 -> 3+2, never 3+1 unless nothing else fits; the extra slides are "(cont.)"). (3) One question size per slide: the largest of 12 -> 11 -> 10.5 pt that fits. (4) Dynamic row heights: content height (minimum 78 pt) plus an equal share of the free height, rows up to 140 pt when the slide has fewer rows; the "Notas / respuesta del cliente" box takes whatever remains, so nothing is left blank. The topic title sits in the arrow band at 16 pt (14 pt, 2 lines max, then truncated at 36 characters with a layout notice). The eyebrow "ETAPA n · VALIDACIÓN" is centred above the stage title. Colours and geometry come from `assets/kickoff-brand.json` (`colores.plan_*`, `plan_accion`): number block petróleo, turquoise bands (the first slightly lighter), menta panel, a white notes box with a turquoise edge, the same palette as the rest of the deck (the grey-blue of the reference was dropped). The `kickoff` format uses these rows only for its grouped validation slides. `--patrones` writes one such slide with 3 rows. The old rounded question cards were removed (nothing else used them). The block divider keeps the dark divider design.

The deck is a first draft of a conversation, not a contract: client-facing wording only; never credentials, never internal doubts (those go to the internal faltantes).

## The faltantes documents
- **Client** (`*-faltantes-cliente.docx`): business language. What they must deliver (`dependencias_cliente` with owner and date), decisions and answers needed (`preguntas_abiertas` of audience `cliente` and **`ambito` negocio**, grouped by stage, with a column for the answer), a line saying the technical questions go to a separate document for their TI team, team data to complete, and the UAT dates rule. It says the agreed scope does not change. It never contains internal doubts, capability checks or the Commercial questions.
- **Technical** (`*-faltantes-tecnico.docx`, «Preguntas para el equipo técnico»; only when there is something technical): for the client's TI team, sent after the meeting. Technical requests (owner, date), every question with **`ambito` tecnico** grouped by integration (`integracion`, or an `INT-n` / `WS#n` / integration name in the text; otherwise by stage) with an answer column, and the documentation / QA status of each integration. No internal or Commercial content.
- **Internal** (`*-faltantes-interno.docx`, "USO INTERNO"): why pre-kickoff (threshold figures), the split of the client questions (N negocio / N técnicas, the ones whose ámbito was deduced by keywords, the mixed ones to split), capabilities to verify and presales doubts (`audiencia: interna`), unconfirmed presales assumptions, **additionals detected (Comercial)**, **changes versus presales**, `alertas_internas`, integration status, Jelou team data, and the **UAT clause marked "borrador: pendiente de revisión por Legal"** plus the 72 h cap note.

## Scope lock in this flow
Normally the client does not ask for extras: **the scope is the presales SOW**. Anything said in the handoff Diio, in the pre-kickoff session (Diio 2), in later meetings or in emails that is not in the presales SOW (`alcance.base`) goes to `ampliaciones_solicitadas[]` with `estado: "por_decidir"`:
- it is NOT in scope: out of scope as "sujeto a evaluación comercial", and the linter errors if its `terminos` appear in flows, stories, rules, solution or effort;
- it produces exactly ONE open question, owner Comercial: "¿Se debe tomar en cuenta este adicional? (requiere aprobación de Comercial y cobro adicional)", visible in the internal faltantes and in the deck's pending slide;
- only `aprobada_comercial` (with `cobro_adicional` and a `cambios` CC-xx row stating the approval) may enter the scope.

Also compare what later meetings say against the presales **assumptions** (for example who does the biometrics, OTP vs facial recognition, retries): list them in `cambios_respecto_preventa` as questions to confirm, never as decisions. Beware follow-up emails that promise an extended scope: record it in `alertas_internas`.

Worked example (anonymized fixture `scripts/test/full-scope-lock-fixture.json`, derived from a credit-origination case): document upload and validation, a dematerialized promissory note, e-signature, installment calculation and a full "credit factory" from the second meeting are five `por_decidir` additions with five Commercial questions; the facial-biometrics-versus-OTP configuration, the retry policy and the test phone line are three changes versus presales with their questions.

## How the template was decompiled (and how to refresh it)
`scripts/dev/extract_kickoff_template.js <example.pptx> <base.pptx>` keeps the theme (colour scheme "Jelou", Poppins), master and layouts (rescaled from 4:3 to 16:9), the five embedded Poppins faces (EOT `.fntdata`) and the media renamed by content (two Jelou logos, 23 line icons: see `assets/kickoff-brand.json` > `iconos`), and drops every slide, the thumbnail, printer settings and document properties. `node scripts/build_kickoff_pptx.js --patrones assets/kickoff-template.pptx` writes the placeholder pattern slides (cover, roadmap, agenda, divider, team, compare, stages, cards, flow, table, rows, questions, risks, pending, next steps, closing, icon gallery) into the template so it can be opened and inspected. The skill needs nothing outside its folder at runtime.

Fonts: Poppins is embedded in the pptx. If the viewer's PowerPoint ignores embedded fonts, it falls back to Arial/Calibri and text looks narrower; install `assets/fonts/Poppins-*.ttf` for exact fidelity.
